DAY 1: Verb-এর পরিচয়

১. Verb কাকে বলে?

যে Word দ্বারা কোনো কাজ করা, হওয়া বা থাকা বোঝায় তাকে Verb বলে।

Examples:
  • Eat = খাওয়া
  • Go = যাওয়া
  • Read = পড়া
  • Write = লেখা
  • Play = খেলা
  • Sleep = ঘুমানো

২. Subject কাকে বলে?

যে ব্যক্তি বা বস্তু সম্পর্কে বাক্যে কিছু বলা হয় তাকে Subject বলে।

Example: Rahim plays football.

Rahim = Subject

৩. Object কাকে বলে?

Verb-এর কাজ যার ওপর পড়ে তাকে Object বলে।

I eat rice.

Rice = Object

৪. Verb-এর প্রকারভেদ

  • Main Verb
  • Helping Verb
  • Transitive Verb
  • Intransitive Verb
  • Finite Verb
  • Non-finite Verb

৫. Transitive Verb

যে Verb-এর Object প্রয়োজন হয় তাকে Transitive Verb বলে।

He eats rice.

৬. Intransitive Verb

যে Verb-এর Object প্রয়োজন হয় না তাকে Intransitive Verb বলে।

The baby sleeps.

Practice

1. He reads a book. Verb কোনটি?

Regular Verb

যেসব Verb-এর V2 ও V3 সাধারণত ed/d যোগ করে তৈরি হয়।

Play - Played - Played

Work - Worked - Worked

Clean - Cleaned - Cleaned

Irregular Verb

যেসব Verb-এর V2 ও V3 নির্দিষ্ট ed নিয়ম অনুসরণ করে না।

Go - Went - Gone

Eat - Ate - Eaten

Write - Wrote - Written

Have Verb

  • I = Have
  • We = Have
  • You = Have
  • They = Have
  • He = Has
  • She = Has

  • I = Do
  • We = Do

  • He = Does
  • She = Does
  • It = Does

Important Rules

Do / Does / Did + V1
Have / Has / Had + V3 (Perfect Tense-এ)
Am / Is / Are + V-ing (Continuous Tense-এ)

Correct: He does not go.

Incorrect: He does not goes.

Correct: She did not eat.

Incorrect: She did not ate.

DAY 4: Present Indefinite Tense

Subject + V1(s/es) + Object

ব্যবহার

  • প্রতিদিনের কাজ
  • অভ্যাস
  • চিরন্তন সত্য
  • সাধারণ ঘটনা
I play football.
He reads books.
The sun rises in the east.
Birds fly in the sky.

Subject অনুযায়ী Verb

SubjectVerb

s/es যোগ করার নিয়ম

  • সাধারণত Verb-এর শেষে s যোগ হয়: Play → Plays
  • s, sh, ch, x, o শেষে থাকলে সাধারণত es: Go → Goes
  • Consonant + y হলে y বাদ দিয়ে ies: Study → Studies
  • Vowel + y হলে শুধু s: Play → Plays

Negative

Subject + do/does + not + V1

Question

Do/Does + Subject + V1?

DAY 5: Present Continuous Tense

Subject + am/is/are + V-ing

বর্তমানে কোনো কাজ চলছে বোঝালে এই Tense হয়।

I am reading.
He is sleeping.
They are playing.
She is cooking.

Helping Verb

SubjectVerb
IAm
He/She/ItIs
You/We/TheyAre

Negative

Subject + am/is/are + not + V-ing

Question

Am/Is/Are + Subject + V-ing?

V-ing বানানোর নিয়ম

  • Play → Playing
  • Make → Making
  • Run → Running
  • Lie → Lying

DAY 6: Present Perfect Tense

Subject + have/has + V3

কাজ শেষ হয়েছে এবং বর্তমানের সঙ্গে সম্পর্ক আছে।

I have eaten rice.
He has gone home.
She has written a letter.
They have finished the work.

Have / Has

  • I, We, You, They = Have
  • He, She, It = Has

Common Words

  • Already
  • Just
  • Yet
  • Ever
  • Never
Have/Has-এর পরে Perfect Tense-এ V3 বসে।

DAY 7: Present Perfect Continuous

Subject + have/has + been + V-ing + since/for

অতীতে শুরু হয়ে এখনো চলছে এমন কাজ।

I have been reading for two hours.
He has been working since morning.

Since

নির্দিষ্ট শুরুর সময় বোঝায়।

  • Since Monday
  • Since 2020
  • Since morning

For

সময় কতক্ষণ ধরে চলছে তা বোঝায়।

  • For two hours
  • For five days
  • For three years

DAY 8: Past Tense-এর চারটি Form

1. Past Indefinite

Subject + V2

I went to school.

2. Past Continuous

Subject + was/were + V-ing

I was reading.

3. Past Perfect

Subject + had + V3

I had eaten rice.

4. Past Perfect Continuous

Subject + had been + V-ing + since/for

I had been reading for two hours.

Was / Were

  • I = Was
  • He/She/It = Was
  • You/We/They = Were
অতীতে দুটি কাজ হলে আগে শেষ হওয়া কাজটি বোঝাতে Past Perfect ব্যবহার হয়।

DAY 9: Future Tense-এর চারটি Form

1. Future Indefinite

Subject + will + V1

I will go.

2. Future Continuous

Subject + will be + V-ing

I will be reading.

3. Future Perfect

Subject + will have + V3

I will have finished.

4. Future Perfect Continuous

Subject + will have been + V-ing

I will have been working.

DAY 11: Subject-Verb Agreement

Rule 1

Singular Subject + Singular Verb

He plays. She reads.

Rule 2

Plural Subject + Plural Verb

They play. Boys read.

Rule 3

Each / Every / Everyone / Everybody / Nobody সাধারণত Singular Verb নেয়।

Everyone is happy.

Each boy has a pen.

Rule 4

One of + Plural Noun + Singular Verb

One of my friends lives here.

Rule 5

A number of + Plural Verb

A number of students are absent.

The number of + Singular Verb

The number of students is increasing.

Rule 6

Many a + Singular Noun + Singular Verb

Many a student has failed.

Rule 7

More than one + Singular Noun + Singular Verb

More than one student is absent.

DAY 12: Special Agreement Rules

Either / Neither

Either of them is ready.

Neither of them is present.

As well as

প্রথম Subject অনুযায়ী Verb বসে।

Rahim as well as his friends is honest.

Along with / Together with

মূল Subject অনুযায়ী Verb বসে।

The teacher along with students is here.

Either...or / Neither...nor

সাধারণত কাছের Subject অনুযায়ী Verb বসে।

Either he or his friends are coming.

Either the boys or Rahim is coming.

Uncountable Noun

Water is essential.

Information is useful.

Advice is important.

DAY 13: Do / Does / Did / Have / Has / Had

Do / Does / Did + V1

Does he go?

Did she eat?

He does not play.

Have / Has / Had + V3 (Perfect Tense-এ)

I have eaten.

She has gone.

They had finished.

ভুল ও সঠিক

Wrong: Does he goes?

Correct: Does he go?

Wrong: Did you went?

Correct: Did you go?

DAY 15: Preposition + V-ing

Preposition + Verb-ing

Preposition-এর পরে Verb এলে সাধারণত V-ing হয়।

  • Fond of reading
  • Interested in learning
  • Good at singing
  • Without saying
  • Before going
  • After eating
  • By working

Look forward to

Look forward to + V-ing

I look forward to meeting you.

Object to

Object to + V-ing

He objected to paying the fee.

সব To-এর পরে ing হয় না। To-infinitive হলে V1 বসে: want to go. Preposition To হলে V-ing হতে পারে: look forward to meeting.

DAY 16: Gerund ও Infinitive

Gerund

Verb + ing যখন Noun-এর কাজ করে।

Swimming is good exercise.

Gerund নেয় এমন Verb

  • Enjoy
  • Avoid
  • Finish
  • Mind
  • Suggest
  • Admit
  • Deny
  • Practice
  • Keep
  • Consider

Example: I enjoy reading.

Infinitive

To + V1

Infinitive নেয় এমন Verb

  • Want
  • Decide
  • Hope
  • Plan
  • Promise
  • Agree
  • Learn
  • Refuse
  • Expect

Example: I want to learn English.

অর্থ পরিবর্তন হতে পারে

Stop smoking = ধূমপান বন্ধ করা।

Stop to smoke = ধূমপান করার জন্য থামা।

DAY 17: Special Verb Rules

Let

Let + Object + V1

Let me go.

Make

Make + Object + V1

He made me laugh.

Passive: I was made to laugh.

Help

Help + Object + V1 / To + V1

He helped me write.

Had better

Had better + V1

You had better study.

Would rather

Would rather + V1

I would rather stay.

Cannot but

Cannot but + V1

I cannot but laugh.

Would you mind

Would you mind + V-ing?

Would you mind opening the door?

DAY 18: Conditional Sentence

Zero Conditional

If + Present, Present

If you heat ice, it melts.

First Conditional

If + Present, Will + V1

If you work hard, you will succeed.

Second Conditional

If + Past, Would + V1

If I were rich, I would help the poor.

Third Conditional

If + Had + V3, Would have + V3

If he had studied, he would have passed.

First Conditional-এ সাধারণত If-clause-এ will বসে না।

Unless

Unless = If not

Unless you study, you will fail.

If you do not study, you will fail.

DAY 19: Passive Voice

Active Voice

Rahim writes a letter.

Passive Voice

A letter is written by Rahim.

TensePassive Structure

Present PerfectHave/Has + been + V3
Past IndefiniteWas/Were + V3
Past ContinuousWas/Were + being + V3
Past PerfectHad + been + V3
Future IndefiniteWill be + V3
ModalModal + be + V3

Examples

The work is done.

The letter was written.

The room is being cleaned.

The work has been completed.

The task will be finished.

DAY 20: Advanced Rules

1. Causative Verb

কাউকে দিয়ে কোনো কাজ করানো বোঝায়।

  • Have + Object + V3
  • Get + Object + V3
  • Make + Person + V1

I had my hair cut.

She got her phone repaired.

2. Present Participle

V-ing

The crying baby is hungry.

3. Past Participle

V3

A broken chair.

4. Perfect Participle

Having + V3

Having finished the work, he went home.

5. Used to

Used to + V1 = অতীতের অভ্যাস।

I used to play football.

6. Be used to

Be used to + Noun / V-ing

I am used to waking early.

7. Get used to

Get used to + Noun / V-ing

He got used to living alone.

8. It is no use

It is no use + V-ing

It is no use crying.

9. There is no

There is no + V-ing

There is no denying the fact.

  8. Want-এর পরে To + V1।
  9. Had better-এর পরে V1।
  10. Would rather-এর পরে V1।
  11. Each/Every সাধারণত Singular Verb নেয়।
  12. One of + Plural Noun + Singular Verb।
